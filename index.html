01.

The response, seen from the road

Brand film

Rapid intervention vehicle

It looks like a pickup. So the film shows it from outside: from the cars and buses it overtakes on the way to a fire.

Made for
East Wind Safety, in my role at Gravity Business Process.
When
Made in seven days, 1 to 7 July 2026. A second, 54-second film of the vehicle followed in the 1800 × 600 banner format for the ADIPEC 2026 stand.
My role
Director. The stills, clips and edit were made in-house.
The problem
A rapid intervention vehicle is a compact fire truck built to reach an incident fast. What it is for only shows on the way to a fire, and that cannot be staged on a public road.
The idea
Follow one response from the station door to the fire, and show it from the cars and buses the vehicle overtakes.
What I decided
  1. Stills before motion. Every shot was settled as a still first; a still is cheap to fix and a clip is not.
  2. One vehicle design held as the reference for every still, so its shape and livery do not drift between shots.
  3. Cut to the beat of the music, so the film keeps its pace on a silent screen.
What happened
Delivered as a 1:04 master with sound, seven days after the first reference image. A banner-format film of the vehicle followed for the ADIPEC 2026 stand.
02.

One stand, one connected story

Exhibition programme

ADIPEC 2026, Abu Dhabi

The content programme for the East Wind stand: a countdown, loops for four LED screen formats, an interactive display and films cut during the show.

Made for
East Wind Safety, in my role at Gravity Business Process. The stand carried Dräger's name beside East Wind's, with products from MIMES, One Seven and xShielder. East Wind represents these manufacturers.
When and where
2 to 5 November 2026. ADNEC, Abu Dhabi. Stand 10730, Hall 10.
My role
Creative director. I set the content plan, briefed the designers and editors, and directed the screen content and the coverage on the floor. The stand itself was built by an outside contractor.
The problem
East Wind sells and services safety equipment made by several manufacturers. On a show floor that can read as a shelf of unrelated products, with no single reason to stop.
The idea
Show the connection instead of listing the products. East Wind's line for the show was one connected safety ecosystem, so the content follows a reading from the sensor to the control room, and each partner's product appears inside an East Wind frame.
What I decided
  1. A master for each screen format, not one stretched file. Type that reads on an 1800-pixel banner is lost on a 400-pixel column.
  2. One visual style for the whole countdown: the same type, logos and booth number every day, with a new product, so the series is recognisable by its second post.
  3. An interactive display beside the films, because the visitors worth a conversation want to try the system.
  4. Coverage cut on the show floor and exported the same day, while a film can still bring someone to the stand.
What happened
Content was delivered for all four screen formats, and a finished film went out on each of the four days of the show.

03.

Follow a reading to the control room

Interactive display

Field digitalization display

An interactive 3D plant that lets a visitor follow one wireless reading from the sensor to the control room, in English and Arabic, with or without a salesperson beside it.

Made for
East Wind Safety, in my role at Gravity Business Process, to explain wireless monitoring technology from MIMES, a manufacturer East Wind represents.
When
From first specification to the stand in fifteen days, 19 October to 2 November 2026.
My role
Concept, experience design, script and creative direction. The software was built by AI coding agents to my briefs.

The display is running here. Select English to try it.

A capture of the display.

Ten applications, each with its own title card, and English and Arabic captions side by side.

The network view, the control room, and English and Arabic on one screen.

The problem
Wireless instrumentation is invisible. A brochure can list ten applications, but an engineer wants to see where the sensor sits and how its signal gets home.
The idea
Let a visitor follow one reading from the sensor to the control room in a live 3D plant, and stop to look around at any point. Left alone, the display runs the tour by itself.
What I decided
  1. A live 3D page instead of a video, so a visitor can stop, choose an application and look around.
  2. Follow one reading hop by hop, from sensor to control room, instead of showing the whole network at once.
  3. English and Arabic captions side by side during the tour, so nobody waits for their language.
  4. Two hand-over packs, because a stand builder and a salesperson need different things.
What happened
It ran on the East Wind stand at ADIPEC 2026; the day-one film shows it in use. On day two of the show an update added the self-running tour. Every technical claim on the screen was checked against a source with East Wind first.

06.

Leading the team

How the work is run

Keeping claims accurate

This is safety equipment, so every claim needs a source. For the field digitalization display, each technical point went to East Wind to confirm; where sources disagreed, the claim was removed. Each post brief carries notes on what the post must not imply.

Building the team

In November 2025 I defined the roles for a graphic designer and a motion editor, and led the recruitment. I now lead three to five people: I brief the work and review it before release.

Generated images and footage

Where a film or image is generated, the work starts from reference images of the real product, I review it before release, and it is labelled as generated. Editing, sound and grade are done the conventional way.
